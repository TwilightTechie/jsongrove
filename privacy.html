Privacy at JSON Grove

Updated 19 October 2026

Your JSON input

The viewer reads, validates, formats, and searches JSON in your browser. JSON Grove does not ask you to create an account and does not send the JSON you paste or open to its server. Your input remains in the current browser session unless you copy or save it yourself.

Site delivery and analytics

Cloudflare hosts this website and may process standard request data, such as your IP address, browser details, and page requests, to deliver and protect the site. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, it may also measure visits and site performance. We do not include your JSON input in analytics events.

Advertising

JSON Grove uses Google AdSense to prepare for and, once approved, display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Third parties may place or read cookies in your browser or use these technologies as a result of ads served on this site. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required, a consent message lets you choose how advertising data is used. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Questions
